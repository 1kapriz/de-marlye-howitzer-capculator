The intention is to have a spotter at a set location on the field. They should measure the distance it is from them to the artillery with a rangefind tool/binocs, then take the bearing of the artillery direction, and plug it in.

Then, they should take the same measurements but to the desired target. The Spotter Height reflects your elevation relative to the field. All measurements are in studs.

Clicks from 0 degrees is how many times you press W to elevate it from flat.
